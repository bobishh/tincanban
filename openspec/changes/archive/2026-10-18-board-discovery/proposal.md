## Why

People with several personal and shared boards need a fast way to identify boards by title and owner and return to work.

## What Changes

- Show board owner in the board picker.
- Add searchable All boards, Mine, and Shared views.
- Add locally pinned boards with manual order and a five-board recent list.
- Keep card search and job-search behavior unchanged.
- Remove confusing extra scroll space from the floating chat while retaining message-list scrolling for long conversations.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `board-discovery`: Search, scope, pin, order, and revisit boards.

## Impact

Board picker UI, local browser preferences, and focused browser coverage. No replicated board data changes.
